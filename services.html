What I do

01Local AI setup

An assistant and document search over your own files — contracts, invoices, manuals, case notes — running on a machine in your office. Ask it questions in plain English and get answers with the source document named. It works with the internet unplugged, because nothing it needs is anywhere else.

02Security checkup

A walk through the things that actually go wrong: passwords and multi-factor sign-in, how your Wi-Fi and router are configured, and a real restore from your backup rather than a green light on a dashboard. You get a one-page written report in plain language — what is fine, what to fix, and what it costs.

03Ongoing care

Monthly maintenance for what is already in place: updates applied, backups verified by restoring from them, and a look at anything that has drifted since last month. The point is that somebody checks on a schedule, so a failed backup is found in a quiet month instead of a bad week.
