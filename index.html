Ph3b3

Made with Soul

Ph3b3 (Phoebe) is a fully local AI assistant. She runs on hardware you already own — no cloud, no data centers, no API calls leaving your network. You talk to her by text or by voice, and every word stays inside your walls.

Privacy here is architecture, not a policy page. A cloud assistant asks you to trust a promise; Ph3b3 removes the wire the data would travel on.
